Daily budgets and repeat protection

Each source has its own JP budget, refreshed at 00:00 UTC every day. This is server reset time, not your local midnight. Your total JP, level, purchases and already claimed rewards do not reset. Unused daily budgets do not carry over, and points beyond a full budget are not queued for tomorrow. If Fishing is capped, you can still earn through the other sources.

Job and mcMMO baselines use your personal highest recorded levels. Relogging, changing Jobs or resetting a skill does not award the same levels again. Biomes and advancements are one-time discoveries per player; revisiting or repeating them does not create a second award. Rejoining also does not refresh a daily budget.

Level calculation, step by step

The first level-up needs 150 JP. Every following step costs 30 JP more: 150, 180, 210, 240 and so on. These are additional JP for that step; the level catalogue shows the cumulative total required.

From Level L to Level L + 1150 + 30 × (L − 1) JPTotal required to reach Level L150 × (L − 1) + 30 × (L − 1) × (L − 2) ÷ 2 JP

How long does the Journey take?

The six budgets add up to an absolute theoretical ceiling of 2,160 JP per UTC day. The current final level requires 213,900 JP. Even 99 maximum-budget days provide only 213,840 JP, so a new player starting at zero needs at least 100 different UTC days through normal progression.

This is a mathematical lower bound, not a promised completion time. Biomes and advancements are finite one-time sources, and repeatedly gaining enough new Job or mcMMO levels becomes harder. The actual Journey can take considerably longer. Players whose old recorded JP were restored already have a head start.

What happens to your previous progress?

The current migration restores proven old JP + JP earned since the new Journey start. It then uses today’s 150/30 curve to determine your new level. Your old numeric level is not copied directly, because the longer curve has different requirements. For example, 5,052 old JP + 22 new JP = 5,074 JP, which is Level 15.

Already purchased permanent features stay owned. Earlier purchase eligibility also stays unlocked, even if your newly calculated level is below today’s requirement. A retained unlock does not turn an unbought feature into a free purchase: its money price and owned predecessor still apply.

How the old progress is reconstructed
  1. If a Journey record from before the restart exists, its saved JP are used directly.
  2. Otherwise the saved old rank record is converted using the original curve: the larger of its old rank minimum and active minutes + 25 × stored old Quest Points.
  3. The chosen old total is added once to your new earned JP. These old sources are alternatives; they are not added together.

For the old rank minimum, old rank 1 maps to former Journey level 1. Later old ranks map to min(116, 12 × (old rank − 1)). The former curve used 30 × (R − 1) + 15 × (R − 1) × (R − 2) ÷ 2 for former Journey level R. That old minimum is used only to reconstruct the historical amount; current levels always use the 150/30 curve.

Example: old rank 4 maps to former level 36, whose old minimum was 9,975 JP. With 6,596 active minutes and 541 stored old Quest Points, 6,596 + 25 × 541 = 20,121 JP. The larger value is 20,121. Add 32 JP earned in the new Journey for 20,153 JP / current Level 33.

This reconstruction does not retrospectively turn your entire lifetime mcMMO, Jobs, fishing or Minecraft playtime totals into new JP. Existing skill levels serve as baselines for future gains. Stored active minutes differ from simply being connected or AFK.

The transfer happens when your profile next loads, normally on join. Previously delivered rewards stay marked as claimed, new earned JP remain, and daily budgets do not reset. Rejoining or restarting does not repeat the historical credit. New players without old recorded progress start at Level 1 / 0 JP.

If your level or a previous purchase appears wrong, tell staff your username and show your Journey/menu tooltip. Staff can check the saved source and purchase record; do not buy the same feature again just to fix a missing display.

Capture a mob as a mob head, carry it with you and release it somewhere else. Each CatchBall has three uses.

Craft a CatchBall

Use a crafting table with 4 diamonds, 4 snowballs and 1 Eye of Ender. Put the snowballs in the four corners, the diamonds above, below and beside the Eye of Ender, and the eye in the centre.

1
Capture

Hold an empty CatchBall and right-click an eligible mob, or throw the ball at it.

2
Carry

Capturing a mob gives you a mob head. Keep the head in your inventory to carry the captured mob.

3
Release

Hold the mob head and right-click a block to release the captured mob.

Quick answers

Why do I still catch ordinary fish?

Custom fish are chance-based. Ordinary fish and Minecraft fishing treasure are still enabled. Specific custom species may also require a different biome, bait, weather or time; check /fish codex.

Why can I not buy bait yet?

Check /fish stats, then compare your Pyro Fishing Level with the bait requirement. You also need enough money. mcMMO Fishing levels do not unlock Pyro bait.

Where is the XP bottle?

Open /fish stats and hover over the left-hand XP bottle. In /fish menu, the “Your Level & Skills” bottle opens your skills.

Should I sell or gut a fish?

Sell it when you want money. Gut it when you want more Entropy for fishing upgrades. First check whether you need the fish for a delivery, bait collection or Totem. A sold or gutted fish is gone.

Why does my contest score not change?

Make sure a contest is running, you are inside the Spawn Fishing region, and your catches match the active challenge. Travel there with /warp fishing.

Custom enchantments add new effects to your equipment. Use to browse them in game, and hover over your item to read its effects.

Thrifty: get spawn eggs and change your spawner

Thrifty I–III is a sword enchantment. Defeat an eligible naturally spawned mob with the sword for a small chance of its spawn egg: I 0.25% · II 0.50% · III 0.75%. It is treasure-only: look for an eligible librarian book trade or generated loot, then use an anvil.

  1. Get the spawn egg of the mob you want.
  2. Hold it and right-click your spawner, aiming at the spawner block.
  3. The egg is consumed and the spawner changes to that mob type. For example, a Zombie Spawn Egg makes it a Zombie Spawner.

You need permission to use the spawner at that location. An egg changes the mob type; it is not a range, speed or spawn-amount upgrade.

No egg farm loop: mobs from normal or trial spawners, breeding, buckets, commands, eggs and other non-natural spawn sources do not count. MythicMobs, the Wither, Ender Dragon and Warden are excluded. A mob must have a usable spawn egg.

Silk Spawner is different: it concerns picking up a mined spawner, while Thrifty provides eggs for changing one. See spawner upgrades and pickup rules.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start painting with ArtMap?

Open /art. The Art Studio contains the recipes, colour palettes and painting help.

Can I buy custom enchanted books from villagers?

Yes. Eligible custom books can appear in librarian trades. Offers are random, and some special enchantments are excluded. Treasure-only enchants are not offered at the enchanting table. See the custom enchantment catalogue for each enchantment’s availability.

Why are my mcMMO and Pyro Fishing levels different?

They are separate progression systems. Open /skills for mcMMO and /fish stats for your Pyro Fishing Level.

Does a shop warp use one of my chest-shop slots?

Public shop warps and chest shops have separate limits. A warp is a teleport destination; a chest shop trades an item.

How long do my items stay in a grave?

A grave lasts three hours. After that, it disappears and its items drop on the ground. Return using or the death coordinates sent in chat.

How do I join on Bedrock?

Add play.townyreborn.com with port 25692. Bedrock support is still in beta. Open the Bedrock instructions →

What do the Journey and perk colours mean?

Path: green is an earlier completed level, yellow your current level, red a future level. Perk menu: green is permanently purchased, blue current rank access, a chest available for purchase, red level-locked. Open the full Journey guide →

Why are NPCs or custom blocks purple and black?

The server resource pack did not load correctly. In the multiplayer list, edit Towny Reborn and set Server Resource Packs to Enabled, then reconnect. If Minecraft offers an old cached pack, close the client before trying again.

Why can I not create an outpost?

Make sure your town has an available outpost slot and at least $3,000 in its bank. Then stand in unclaimed wilderness on Earth, in the Earth Nether or in the Earth End and use /t claim outpost. Outposts have no maximum distance from your existing town land.

Can I build a base in a resource world?

You technically can place temporary blocks, but the worlds reset every day at 06:00 UTC and permanent systems such as homes and claims are blocked. Build your real base on Earth.

What happens if my town cannot pay upkeep?

If the town bank cannot cover the daily upkeep, the town immediately becomes a ruin; there is no debt or bankruptcy grace period. Its claims remain reserved for up to 48 hours, but the town is controlled by an NPC mayor, can be griefed, and most town commands and all plot commands are disabled.

After the first hour, a town resident can restore it with /t reclaim for $500. If nobody reclaims it within 48 hours, the town is permanently deleted and its claims return to wilderness. Deposit money beforehand with /t deposit <amount> to prevent this.
